# How strong the evidence is

Generated by `python3 scripts/evidence_stats.py` from each model's `export/runs.csv`, and for the
first model call from its task batch's `runs.jsonl`. Every task ran five times on each server.
A run's tokens are its complete-task tokens, and its success is the verifier's grade. A ratio is
the endpoint server's median over the workflow server's for one task, and paired runs share a
task and a repetition. Intervals are 95%: Wilson intervals for a share of runs, and percentile
bootstrap intervals that resample tasks 10,000 times (seed 20261106), since runs of one task
aren't independent. Token counts can't be compared across providers.
